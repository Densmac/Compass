# Release notes

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Compass dial that keeps north pointing north, with true and magnetic north
- Fling the wheel: momentum from your flick, landing exactly on the real heading
- Haptic detents and tick sounds, stronger at 30° marks and cardinal points
- Latitude, longitude and elevation above sea level
- Bubble level tab
- Dark and light appearance
- Settings for north reference, haptics, tick sounds, units, coordinates and keep screen on

Requires Android 12 or newer.
